# Privacy Policy for Space Invaders

**Last updated: March 2026**

## Overview

Space Invaders is a desktop game application. This privacy policy explains how the application handles user data.

## Data We Collect

- **Player Name**: If you choose to log in with Facebook, your Facebook display name is used as your in-game player name for the current session only.
- **Game Scores**: Your scores are saved locally on your device in a file named `scores.db` in the game's installation folder. This data is never transmitted to any server.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.
- We do not access your Facebook profile beyond your display name.
- We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
- We do not share any data with third parties.

## Facebook Login

Facebook Login is used solely to retrieve your display name for use within the game. No Facebook data is stored beyond the current session.

## Contact

This is a student project. For questions, contact the developer via the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Avital-Fine/space-invaders).
